## History of computers:
Computers evolved from simple calculating devices to digital systems.
The history of computers is divided into five generations , from vacuum tubes to artificial intelligence.
Below is a description of five generations of computers in a table format:
|Generation|Technology|example|
|---|---|---|
|first|vacuum tubes|ENIAC|
|second|transistors|IBM 1401|
|third|ICs|IBM 360|
|fourth|microprocessor|PCs|
|fifth|AI and advanced computing|modern ai systems|

## Types of computers:
Computers can be categorized on the basis of :
* Purpose
* size
* data handling
### Purpose:
There are two types of computers on the basis of purpose.
1. General purpose computers
2. Special purpose computers
### Size:
There are four main types of computers on the basis of size:
1. Microcomputers
2. Minicomputers
3. Mainframe computers
4. Supercomputers
### Data handling:
There are three types of computers on this basis:
1. Analog Computers
2. Digital Computers
3. Hybrid Computers
